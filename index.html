How to play

  • Get all 3 balls into the green goal. A ball in a red-rimmed hole drops out and restarts at the blue ring.
  • Levels 1–2 are untimed. From level 3 there is a clock; later levels add offset holes, corner holes on turns and orange sliding gates you must time.
  • ★★★ = no falls and fast · ★★ = up to 2 falls · ★ = cleared.
  • Gyro: hold the phone however is comfortable — the countdown levels it. Tap 🎯 Re-level any time. iPhone asks for motion permission on first play.
  • Gesture: touch and drag anywhere on the board — the drag distance is the tilt.

3D viewing modes

  • Parallel 3D: left image for left eye — cardboard viewers or parallel free-viewing.
  • Cross-Eye 3D: cross your eyes until a third image fuses in the middle.
  • Anaglyph: red/cyan glasses.
  • Smart Glasses (HSBS): 16:9 half side-by-side for RayNeo Air, XREAL, Rokid, TCL — switch the glasses to 3D SBS mode.
  • 🥽 VR: appears in Meta Quest browser; the board floats on a virtual table, thumbstick tilts it.
  • 3D Depth scales automatically with board size; lower it if your eyes strain. Focus moves the screen plane.
